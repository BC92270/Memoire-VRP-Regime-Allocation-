\section{Robustness Grid and Supplementary Diagnostics}
\label{app:robustness}

The main text reports the economically relevant numerical results from the robustness analysis. This appendix documents the complete design of the parameter grid and the interpretation applied consistently across markets. The full machine-readable output grids are retained in the project under \texttt{outputs/tables/}, allowing every parameter combination to be inspected without reproducing several hundred rows in the manuscript.

The direct-variance crisis exclusions use fixed calendar windows:
the Global Financial Crisis from 1 September 2008 to 30 June 2009;
the Euro Sovereign Crisis from 1 July 2011 to 31 July 2012;
Volmageddon from 1 February 2018 to 31 March 2018;
the Covid Shock from 1 February 2020 to 31 May 2020; and
the Inflation and Rate Shock from 1 January 2022 to 31 October 2022.
The joint crisis exclusion removes the union of these windows.

For the selected United States High-VRP strategy, transaction costs are not the principal source of fragility: its Sharpe ratio moves only from approximately 0.885 at zero stylized cost to 0.869 at 50 basis points. The more important weaknesses are the 12-month risk-estimation specification, which produces a Sharpe ratio of 0.578 and a maximum drawdown of approximately -50.7\%, and the substantial differences across subperiods. Mean--variance certainty-equivalent differences remain negative relative to both balanced benchmarks for $\gamma$ from one through five and turn only slightly positive at $\gamma=10$.

The European VRP-positive strategy is substantially more stable inside the model-based approximation. Its Sharpe ratio remains approximately 1.303 at 50 basis points of stylized monthly cost. All tested risk-estimation windows remain economically strong on their common aligned sample. Reducing the maximum notional to 5\% still produces a Sharpe ratio of approximately 1.482. Performance remains positive in both sample halves and in the pre- and post-Covid partitions. Joint exclusion of all designated major-crisis observations leaves 141 observations and increases the Sharpe ratio to approximately 1.877. Mean--variance certainty-equivalent differences remain positive for every tested value of $\gamma$.

These robustness results remain conditional on the payoff approximation described in Section~3. They do not convert the modeled return series into an observed variance-swap return and do not incorporate exact option-replication costs, collateral, margin, dealer spreads, or intramonth mark-to-market constraints.
